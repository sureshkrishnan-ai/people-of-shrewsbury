Built a city on the People of City foundation? We want to see it.

Exceptional projects may be invited to collaborate on the larger People of City ecosystem. **Submitting doesn't guarantee inclusion**: every submission is read, but only some will be selected.

## How to submit

Post in **[Discussions → Show and tell](https://github.com/peopleofcity/peopleofcity/discussions)** with the title **`City: People of <Your City>`**, and include:

1. **City name:** e.g. People of Chennai
2. **Project URL:** where we can walk through it
3. **GitHub repository:** your fork, public, under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE)
4. **Screenshots or a video:** at least two screenshots, or a short screen recording (30–90 seconds is perfect)
5. **Short description:** what your city is, in two or three sentences
6. **What makes it unique:** the landmarks, the look, the sound, the features you added
7. **About you:** your name or handle, and how to reach you (an email address or a social handle)

Keep private details out of a public post. If you'd rather share contact details privately, say so in the post and we'll reach out.

## How we look at submissions

| | What we're looking for |
|---|---|
| **Quality of execution** | It runs well on a laptop and a phone, and nothing feels broken or unfinished |
| **Originality** | It feels like *your* city, not Bengaluru with a new name |
| **Visual experience** | A consistent, intentional look. See [`design.md`](design.md) for the bar |
| **Functionality** | What works, and anything new you built on top of the foundation |
| **Community potential** | Would people in your city actually come here and meet each other? |
| **Overall experience** | Would someone walk around for ten minutes without being asked to? |

## What happens next

- We read every submission and may reply in the thread with questions.
- Selected creators may be invited to collaborate on the People of City ecosystem: a shout-out, a feature, or building together.
- Improvements to the shared foundation that came out of your city (a better building kit, a new tool) are welcome as pull requests here: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Ground rules

- Your city must follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and the [license](LICENSE): if it's public, its source is public too.
- Only show real people who agreed to be in it. Sample residents must be fictional.
- Don't use the People of City, People of Bengaluru or People of SF names or logos in a way that suggests your city is official before it's selected.
