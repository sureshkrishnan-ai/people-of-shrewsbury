# Owner interview template

For students and volunteers talking to a local business owner (the Girl Scout local-economy project, school projects).
Ask the owner first whether they'd like to be listed; nothing is published without their signed or online consent
(`docs/consent/business-v1.md`). Interview in pairs, in public business hours, with a parent/leader present for minors.

## Before
- Introduce yourself (first names only) and the project: a community map of local businesses.
- Ask permission to take notes. Ask separately before any photo; never photograph customers or staff who say no.

## Questions (pick 6–8)
1. What does your business do, in one sentence?
2. When and why did it start here in Shrewsbury?
3. Who started it? Is it a family business?
4. How many people work here? (optional — fine to skip)
5. Where do your customers come from — mostly town, or further?
6. Do you buy from other local businesses (suppliers, services)? Which kinds?
7. What's the hardest part of running a small business?
8. What's changed on this street since you opened?
9. What kind of business do you think the town is missing?
10. What would you tell a kid who wants to start a business?
11. Is there a story or photo you'd like on your listing?

## After
- Read back the short story you'd publish (≤ 1200 characters) and let them correct it.
- They submit it (or confirm it) through the "For businesses" page, which records consent.
- Thank-you note within a week.

## Notes for the economy project
- Tally answers to 5 and 6 to talk about the *local multiplier* (money re-spent in town).
- Compare answer 9 with the Ideas board on `/economy` — does the map agree with the owners?
