# Business listing consent — version business-v1

Shown on the business form (source of truth: `src/city/consent.ts`); the version id (`business-v1`) is stored with every listing (`businesses.consent_version`).
Change the wording → make a new file `business-v2.md` and bump the version in the form. Never edit a published version.

> I am the owner of this business, or I am authorised by the owner to list it.
>
> I agree that People of Shrewsbury may show the information and photos I provide here — the business name, category,
> address, website, story, year founded, number of jobs (optional), my name (only if I choose to show it) and my photos —
> on its public website and in its 3D town, and may use them in the community/Girl Scout local-economy project that
> runs it.
>
> I confirm the photos are mine to share and show no one who hasn't agreed to appear. I won't include anything about
> customers, children, security arrangements or private areas.
>
> I can change or remove my listing at any time by signing in, or by emailing the maintainers; removal happens within
> 7 days. The project may decline or edit a listing that breaks its community rules.
